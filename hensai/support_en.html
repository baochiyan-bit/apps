Frequently asked questions

The balance in the app doesn't match my statement.
Interest is calculated as balance × APR ÷ 365 × days. Lenders round differently, so small differences can appear. When you record a payment, enter the "Interest (from statement)" or "Balance after (from statement)" figures and the app recalculates from your statement's numbers.
How do I change the currency?
Go to Settings → Region & currency. The currency defaults to your device's region. Changing it only changes the unit shown; amounts you've already entered are not converted.
How do I move to a new iPhone?
Use Settings → Export backup to save a file, then choose Restore from backup on the new iPhone. If iCloud sync is on, your data follows you automatically when you sign in with the same Apple ID.
How do I delete my data?
Deleting the app removes all data on the device. If iCloud sync was on, you can also delete the app's data from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone.
How do I remove ads?
Go to Settings → Go Premium and make the one-time purchase. If you reinstall the app, tap Restore purchase.
What happens when a due date falls on a weekend?
In Settings → "When a date falls on a weekend or holiday" you can choose no change, previous business day or next business day. Follow your lender's rule. Japanese public holidays can be turned on in Region & currency.
I don't receive reminders.
Check that "Due date reminders" is on in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for the app in your iPhone's Settings.
