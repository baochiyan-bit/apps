Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled in Debt Payoff (Japanese name: 返済管理; the "App"). It applies to both the iPhone version and the Android version of the App.

1. Basic policy

The operator stores the information you enter in the App on your device. Except as described in this policy, it is not sent outside your device or shared with third parties.

2. Information handled and purposes

InformationPurposeWhere it is stored
Lender names, balances, interest rates, due dates, minimum payments, payment and borrowing records, simulation settingsCalculating and displaying balances, interest and payoff dates; building a repayment planOn your device (in your own iCloud if you turn on iCloud sync in the iPhone version)

The App does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address, phone number or postal address. No account is required.

3. Where data is stored

Information entered in the App is stored on your device. It is not sent to any server operated by the operator.

In the Android version, the App's data is excluded from Android's automatic backup to your Google account. However, if you use a device-to-device transfer when you switch phones, the App's data may be copied to the new device.

4. iCloud sync (iPhone version only)

If you turn on iCloud sync in the settings of the iPhone version, your data is stored in your own private area of iCloud (CloudKit), provided by Apple Inc., and synced between devices signed in with the same Apple ID.

Only your Apple ID can access this data; the operator cannot view or retrieve it. For how Apple handles data in iCloud, see Apple's Privacy Policy (https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

The Android version has no sync feature. Your data stays only on that device.

5. Notifications

The App's reminders are local notifications created on your device. No information is sent anywhere to deliver them.

6. Biometric lock

On iPhone, the lock feature uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. On Android, it uses your device's fingerprint or face unlock, or your screen lock. Authentication is handled by the operating system; the App never receives or stores biometric data such as your face or fingerprint.

7. Widgets

The summary shown in the widgets is stored in a shared area on your device used by the App and its widgets. It is not sent anywhere.

8. Calendar export

Only if you turn it on in the settings, the App adds your due dates to a calendar on your device. The events go into a separate calendar that the App creates for this purpose.

This requires calendar access. The App only adds and removes events in that calendar. It does not read or change events in your other calendars, and it does not send event details anywhere.

9. Backup files

If you export a backup, the file is saved to the location you choose: on iPhone, the Files app, iCloud Drive and so on; on Android, a folder on your device, Google Drive or another location you pick in the file picker. You are responsible for managing that file.

10. Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To serve ads, Google may collect the information listed above.

On iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is used for ads tailored to your interests only if you allow it in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone.

On Android, Google uses your Android advertising ID to serve ads. You can reset or delete the advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (on some devices, Settings → Google → Ads).

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a consent screen provided by Google is shown before any ad is displayed, on both iPhone and Android. You can change your choices at any time from the App's Settings ("Ad privacy settings").

The loans, records and other information you enter in the App are never sent for advertising purposes.

For how Google handles information, see Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its advertising page (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

11. In-app purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple Inc.'s App Store on iPhone and by Google LLC's Google Play on Android. The operator never receives payment details such as credit card numbers.

12. Sharing with third parties

The operator does not share your information with third parties except where required by law.

The App uses the following external service:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): ad delivery — https://policies.google.com/privacy

13. Deleting your data

Because the App's data is stored on your device, deleting (uninstalling) the App removes all of your records, on both iPhone and Android. Deleted data cannot be recovered. If you used iCloud sync in the iPhone version, you can delete the App's data from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone. Backup files you exported are not deleted with the App; delete them from where you saved them if you no longer need them.

14. Children

The App is not intended for use by children under 13.

15. Changes to this policy

This policy may change in response to changes in the law or new features of the App. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on this page.
