Debt Payoff — Support

Keep every loan and credit card in one place, see when you'll be debt-free, and compare payoff methods. Japanese name: 返済管理.

Frequently asked questions

The balance in the app doesn't match my statement.
By default, interest is calculated as balance × APR ÷ 365 × days. Lenders round differently, so small differences can appear. When you record a payment, enter the "Interest (from statement)" or "Balance after (from statement)" figures and the app recalculates from your statement's numbers.
Can the app match how loans work in my country?
Yes. In Settings → Region & currency, choose your country or region (Thailand, Indonesia, Vietnam, China, Saudi Arabia, United Arab Emirates, Egypt and others). New loans then start with the usual setup there, such as flat-rate interest or a credit card minimum payment set as a percentage of the balance, and the weekend days used for due dates. You can change these for each loan to match your contract or statement. Loans you've already added are not changed.
What is a flat rate?
With a flat rate, interest is charged on the original amount borrowed (original amount × APR ÷ 12 each month), so it stays the same even as your balance goes down. Many installment and personal loans are quoted this way. Choose "Flat rate" under Interest method when you add a loan, and enter the original amount.
How do I change the currency?
Go to Settings → Region & currency. The currency defaults to your device's region. Changing it only changes the unit shown; amounts you've already entered are not converted.
How do I move to a new phone?
Use Settings → Export backup to save a file, then choose Restore from backup on the new phone. Save the file somewhere the new phone can open it, such as Google Drive, iCloud Drive or email. Backup files use the same format on iPhone and Android, so you can also use them to move from iPhone to Android or from Android to iPhone. Between iPhones, if iCloud sync is on, your data follows you automatically when you sign in with the same Apple ID. The Android version doesn't store data in the cloud, so use a backup file.
Can I sync between iPhone and Android?
No. iCloud sync works only between iPhones, and the Android version has no sync feature. To move data between iPhone and Android, use a backup file.
Can I add due dates to my calendar?
Yes. Turn on calendar export in the app's settings, and your due dates are added to a separate calendar that the app creates (the same on iPhone and Android). The app asks for calendar access the first time. It only adds and removes events in that calendar and never reads or changes your other calendars.
How do I delete my data?
Deleting (uninstalling) the app removes all data on the device. If iCloud sync was on in the iPhone version, you can also delete the app's data from Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage on your iPhone. Backup files you exported stay after you delete the app, so delete them from where you saved them if you don't need them.
How do I remove ads?
Go to Settings → Go Premium and make the one-time purchase. If you reinstall the app, tap Restore purchase while signed in with the same Apple ID (on Android, the same Google account) you bought it with. Purchases are separate on the App Store and Google Play, so Premium doesn't carry over between iPhone and Android.
What happens when a due date falls on a weekend?
In Settings → "When a date falls on a weekend or holiday" you can choose no change, previous business day or next business day. Follow your lender's rule. The weekend days follow the country or region you choose (for example, Friday and Saturday in Saudi Arabia and Egypt). Japanese public holidays can be turned on in Region & currency.
I don't receive reminders.
Check that "Due date reminders" is on in the app's Settings, and that notifications are allowed for the app in your phone's settings (iPhone: Settings → the app → Notifications; Android: Settings → Apps → the app → Notifications). On Android, if reminders arrive late or not at all, turning off battery optimization for the app may help.
