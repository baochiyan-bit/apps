FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still stuck? Email us.
See also the support page.

The balance in the app is a little different from my statement.
Interest is calculated as balance × annual rate ÷ 365 × days, so rounding differences between lenders can cause small gaps. When you record a payment, enter the interest or the balance after payment from your statement, and the app recalculates from those figures.
Can I use it for loans with an interest-free period?
Yes. Enter the contract date and the number of interest-free days, and the app uses the promotional rate for that period and the normal rate afterwards. If the rate changes later, you can add the date and the new rate.
How do I move to a new phone?
Go to Settings → Export backup to save a file, then choose Restore from backup on the new phone. Backup files use the same format on iPhone and Android, so you can also switch between iPhone and Android. Between iPhones with iCloud sync on, your data follows you when you sign in with the same Apple ID.
Is Premium a subscription?
No. It's a one-time purchase. After you buy it, use Restore purchase on any device signed in to the same Apple ID (on Android, the same Google account). App Store and Google Play purchases are separate, so Premium doesn't carry over between iPhone and Android.
Will the app try to sell me loans or refinancing?
No. The app is for managing and calculating your repayments. It doesn't promote or advise on borrowing or refinancing. Results are estimates, so check the actual amounts on your lender's statements.

You don't have to handle it alone.

If repayments become hard to manage, talk to your lender early, or contact a free, non-profit debt advice service in your country. The sooner you ask, the more options you'll have.
